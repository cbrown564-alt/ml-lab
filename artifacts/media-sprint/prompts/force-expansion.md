# Phase-two recurring-force expansion

All four jobs used `masters/force-reference-sheet-v1.png` as the exact style and identity reference. Each requested six small action or habit states in the selected matte cut-paper language. Shared constraints: character-like silhouettes without faces or limbs; no text; no exact datasets, equations, axes, or technical geometry; secondary narrative use only.

- **Optimizer:** survey, descend, measured step, overshoot, diminishing oscillation, stable rest.
- **Noise:** barely present, ordinary variation, one outlier, contamination, obscured signal, reduced interference.
- **Regularizer:** absent, light guidance, balance, excessive constraint, partial release, stable simple path.
- **Curse of Dimensionality:** compact neighbors, progressive separation and empty space, then partial repopulation with more observations.
